2. Tap **Play** > Go to the **Servers** tab.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the list and tap **Add Server**.
4. Fill in the boxes exactly as follows:
   * **Server Name:** `Wixfox Network`
   * **Server Address:** `Professor002.aternos.me` *(Make sure there is NO `https://` and NO `:port` in this box!)*

5. Tap **Save** or **Play** to join the server!
